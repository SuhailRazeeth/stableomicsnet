\section{Supplementary Data}

All primary data analyzed are publicly available. TCGA-BRCA and ROSMAP are available in the preprocessed form distributed with MOGONET, TCGA KIRC, KIRP and KICH from UCSC Xena, and METABRIC from the cBioPortal data hub. The StableOmicsNet source code, the KIPAN construction script, the simulation code and the complete result tables reproduced below are available at \url{https://github.com/SuhailRazeeth/stableomicsnet} and archived at \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22348567}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.22348567}.
